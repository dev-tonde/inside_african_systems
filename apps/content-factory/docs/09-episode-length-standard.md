# Episode length standard

There is no universal ideal YouTube length. The correct duration is the shortest cut that fully delivers the promise without rushing the explanation. Audience retention and satisfaction matter more than reaching a round number.

## Inside African Systems target

- Default documentary: **8–12 minutes**
- Complex investigation with strong evidence: **10–14 minutes**
- Focused explainer or pilot: **5–8 minutes**, used intentionally
- Shorts and excerpts: separate formats, not compressed documentaries

At a calm 125–145 spoken words per minute, an 8–12 minute episode normally needs roughly 1,100–1,600 spoken words after allowing time for chapter breaths, charts, and visual comprehension. This is a planning range, not a quota.

## Why the Sixty60 pilot is 5:32

The approved scene script contains 901 spoken words. The first render also used a fast synthetic delivery and placed independently generated narration clips directly against one another, which produced the rushed 5:17 cut and awkward joins.

The corrected cut uses a 0.96 narration speed, edge protection on every generated clip, 0.65-second chapter pads, and a 1.5-second closing hold. That fixes pacing and increases the duration to 5:32. It cannot create three additional minutes of substantive documentary material.

## Expansion rule

Never slow narration unnaturally or repeat claims to reach eight minutes. Expand only through sourced material that strengthens the audience promise. For this episode, the best additions would be:

1. A store-level walkthrough from order placement to hand-off.
2. What the public performance metrics measure—and what they do not prove.
3. The role of existing supermarket infrastructure versus dedicated dark stores.
4. Competitive context in South African on-demand grocery.
5. Transferable lessons and limits for retailers elsewhere in Africa.

The longer version needs an updated evidence ledger, approved script, storyboard, narration, captions, and replacement QA report before public release.
