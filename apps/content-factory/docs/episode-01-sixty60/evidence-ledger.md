## Gate

- `thesis_status`: Supported.
- `missing_evidence`: Standalone profit, exact allocation algorithm, independently audited delivery performance.
- `high_risk_claims`: Worker compensation and protections; standalone profitability.
- `recommended_scope_changes`: State that profitability is undisclosed. Attribute employment and benefit descriptions. Do not estimate confidential unit economics.
