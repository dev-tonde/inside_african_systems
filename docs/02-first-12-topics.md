# First 12 documentary topics

## Method

YouTube does not publish trustworthy public keyword-volume figures for ordinary researchers. These rankings therefore use observable demand proxies as of July 2026:

- Recognizability and frequency of public searches
- Existing video and news coverage
- Recurring public questions
- Current business relevance
- Availability of primary evidence
- Visual-story potential
- Evergreen value
- Competition gap for a polished African visual documentary

The score is a comparative editorial estimate, not a claim about exact monthly searches.

## Demand order versus publication order

Eskom has the highest estimated raw demand, but it should not be Episode 1. It is technically and politically harder, carries more claim risk, and will expose weaknesses in a new workflow.

Sixty60 is the best pilot because it has:

- A clear viewer question
- Familiar everyday stakes
- Strong primary reporting from Shoprite
- Easy-to-understand system diagrams
- Limited need for historical footage
- Lower legal and political risk
