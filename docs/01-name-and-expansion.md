# Name and expansion decision

## Verdict

Do not launch the master channel as **How Mzansi Works**.

The July 2026 search did not surface an obvious exact-name YouTube channel, but that is not enough to make the brand safe:

- A South African economics book published in 2025 already uses the title *How Mzansi Works (and Sometimes Doesn’t)*.
- “Mzansi” permanently signals South Africa, while the intended subject naturally expands to the continent.
- A later rename would weaken recognition, search history, visual consistency, and word-of-mouth.

Search evidence: [report about the existing book](https://www.citizen.co.za/randfontein-herald/news-headlines/local-news/2025/11/17/read-author-simplifies-township-economics-with-humour/).

## Recommended working name

**Inside African Systems**

Positioning line:

> The companies, infrastructure, markets and ideas that move Africa.

The name is descriptive enough to understand, broad enough for 54 countries, and specific enough to establish an editorial territory. Public searches did not surface an obvious exact-name YouTube channel or dominant media brand at the observation date. This is not a trademark clearance or a guarantee that the YouTube handle remains available.

Before launch:

1. Attempt to create the exact YouTube handle.
2. Search CIPC and relevant trademark databases.
3. Check domain and social handles.
4. Reserve the accounts on the same day.
5. Do not order paid assets until the checks are complete.

## Expansion model

The channel name remains unchanged. Only seasons and playlists change:

- Season 1: South Africa
- Season 2: East African Systems
- Season 3: West African Systems
- Season 4: North African Systems
- Season 5: Systems Connecting Africa

Use country labels in episode packaging:

- `SOUTH AFRICA · S01`
- `KENYA · S02`
- `NIGERIA · S03`

This lets a South African launch remain focused without creating a South-Africa-only brand.
